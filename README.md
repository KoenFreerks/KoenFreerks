# Hey there! I'm Koen 👋

**Computational Ethologist & PhD Candidate at the University of Groningen**

I'm a behavioural ecologist interested in evolutionary biology, alternative reproductive tactics, and the use of computational methods to understand animal behaviour.

My work spans **behavioural experiments, computer vision, automated tracking, and quantitative analysis**, bridging biological questions with the development of new experimental and analytical tools.

I enjoy working across disciplines and collaborating on diverse research questions, organisms, and datasets. Alongside my PhD, I contribute to collaborative projects in different areas of biology while developing software and experimental systems for my own research. I value **open, reproducible, and accessible research tools** that can benefit the wider scientific community.

### Research

My PhD investigates the **eco-evolutionary dynamics of alternative reproductive tactics in male two-spotted spider mites** (*Tetranychus urticae*), exploring how behavioural variation, ecological conditions, and evolutionary processes interact.

My broader research interests include:

- Alternative reproductive tactics, fighting behaviour, and behavioural polymorphisms
- Eco-evolutionary dynamics and sexual selection
- AI-driven posture tracking and automated behavioural classification
- Computer vision and custom imaging for behavioural experiments
- Social, reproductive, and sleep behaviour in *Drosophila melanogaster*

A major component of my PhD involves developing the experimental and computational infrastructure to study animals just **0.5 mm in length**. I'm building a custom imaging system for spider mites alongside an automated analysis pipeline that takes recordings from **video, to posture, to behaviour**.

My PhD comprises multiple interconnected research questions, with further chapters and papers in development. I am also contributing to additional collaborative manuscripts on lizard limb variation and *Drosophila* reproductive and sleep behaviour.

### Research Software & Tools

I develop software and hardware to make behavioural experiments and analysis more efficient, reproducible, and accessible. My interests extend beyond applying existing methods to **building practical tools that address the technical challenges of biological research**.

- [**ocelli**](https://github.com/KoenFreerks/ocelli) — camera monitoring and control for behavioural experiments.
- [**stylet**](https://github.com/KoenFreerks/stylet) — video preprocessing for downstream behavioural analysis.
- **Movement & behaviour tracking** — developing a next-generation, universal tracking system to connect video recordings with quantitative movement and behavioural data across organisms and experimental setups.
- **Additional project in development** — more details when the time is right.

I aim to make research software well-documented, practical to use, and reusable wherever possible. My existing projects are open source, and I value the transparency and collaborative development this enables.
